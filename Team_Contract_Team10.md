## Introduction
This teamwork contract outlines the agreed-upon expectations, responsibilities, and
guidelines for effective collaboration among the members of Team 10. By adhering to this
contract, we aim to foster a productive, respectful, and supportive team environment
conducive to the success of our project.
## Team Expectations
## 1. Participation
## • Expectation:
o Actively participate in all team meetings and discussions.
o Engage fully in project activities and contribute ideas.
o Notify the team in advance if they are unable to attend a meeting.
- Contribution (Progress on the Project)
## • Expectation:
o Complete assigned tasks correctly and on time.
o Contribute equal effort to the project's progress.
o Willing to share information and help when needed.
## 3. Communication
## • Expectation:
o Professional, respectful, and considerate.
o Contact on preferred platform (Text or Microsoft teams)
o Inform members which platform you contact on.

o Responsive, with replies to inquiries within 24 hours on weekdays.
o Not expected to respond on weekends
## 4. Meetings
## • Expectation:
o Frequency: The team will decide the previous week when to meet, at a
minimum of twice a week
o Attendance: Members are expected to attend all meetings unless
unforeseen circumstances occur
o Members should come prepared, having completed any agreed upon
tasks or research.
o Can change meeting times based on team agreement
## 5. Conduct
## • Expectation:
o Treat each other with respect and courtesy.
o Listen actively and allow others to express their ideas without interruption.
o Address disagreements professionally and constructively.
## 6. Conflict Resolution
## • Expectation:
o Address conflicts in person (preferred) or through text (secondary)
o Use these steps to resolve issues:
▪ Discuss the issue privately between involved parties.
▪ If unresolved, bring the issue to the team for discussion.
▪ Seek input from the instructor if necessary.
o Focus on finding mutually acceptable solutions.
o Warning Process for Non-Performance:
▪ Direct Discussion: The team will hold an internal meeting with the
member to review missed expectations and establish clear, written
action items.

▪ Written Warning: If performance does not improve within 3–5 days,
the Team Leader will issue a formal internal warning outlining
specific deliverables required.
▪ Instructor Escalation: If non-performance continues past the
warning period, the team will submit all documented task histories
and communication logs to the course instructor for intervention.
o Resolve conflict before instructor or advisor meetings
## 7. Deadlines
## • Expectation:
o All team and instructor deadlines are to be met.
o If a member has a problem meeting a deadline, contact the team and let
them know.
o The team will adjust workloads to ensure project timelines are maintained
o Have presentations or important documents that need to be presented to
an instructor or advisor completed 24 hours in advance
