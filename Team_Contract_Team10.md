## Introduction

This teamwork contract outlines the agreed-upon expectations, responsibilities, and guidelines for effective collaboration among the members of Team 10. By adhering to this contract, we aim to foster a productive, respectful, and supportive team environment conducive to the success of our project.

---

## Team Expectations

### 1. Participation
* **Actively participate** in all team meetings and discussions.
* **Engage fully** in project activities and contribute ideas.
* **Notify the team in advance** if unable to attend a meeting.

### 2. Contribution (Progress on the Project)
* **Complete assigned tasks** correctly and on time.
* **Contribute equal effort** to the project's progress.
* **Be willing to share information** and help team members when needed.

### 3. Communication
* **Tone:** Professional, respectful, and considerate.
* **Platforms:** Contact via preferred platforms (Text or Microsoft Teams). Inform members which platform you are contacting them on.
* **Responsiveness:** Responsive with replies to inquiries within **24 hours on weekdays**.
* **Weekends:** Members are not expected to respond on weekends.

### 4. Meetings
* **Frequency:** The team will decide the previous week when to meet, at a minimum of **twice a week**.
* **Attendance:** Members are expected to attend all meetings unless unforeseen circumstances occur.
* **Preparation:** Members should come prepared, having completed any agreed-upon tasks or research.
* **Flexibility:** Meeting times can change based on team agreement.

### 5. Conduct
* Treat each other with **respect and courtesy**.
* **Listen actively** and allow others to express their ideas without interruption.
* Address disagreements **professionally and constructively**.

### 6. Conflict Resolution
* **Communication Channel:** Address conflicts in person (preferred) or through text (secondary).
* **Resolution Steps:**
  1. **Direct Discussion:** Discuss the issue privately between involved parties.
  2. **Team Discussion:** If unresolved, bring the issue to the entire team for discussion.
  3. **Instructor Input:** Seek input from the instructor if necessary.
  4. **Goal:** Focus on finding mutually acceptable solutions.

* **Warning Process for Non-Performance**
  1. **Direct Discussion:** The team will hold an internal meeting with the member to review missed expectations and establish clear, written action items.
  2. **Written Warning:** If performance does not improve within **3–5 days**, the Team Leader will issue a formal internal warning outlining specific deliverables required.
  3. **Instructor Escalation:** If non-performance continues past the warning period, the team will submit all documented task histories and communication logs to the course instructor for intervention.
> *Note: Resolve conflicts before instructor or advisor meetings.*

### 7. Deadlines
* All team and instructor deadlines are to be met.
* If a member has a problem meeting a deadline, they must **contact the team immediately**. The team will adjust workloads to ensure project timelines are maintained.
* Have presentations or important documents that need to be presented to an instructor or advisor completed **24 hours in advance**.

* **Responsibilities:**

  * Facilitate meetings to ensure all voices are heard.
  * Monitor project timelines and milestones.
* **Powers Granted:**
  * Authority to make minor decisions to keep the project on track after team consultation.
* **Expectation Changes:**
  * In addition to regular duties, the Team Leader will handle organizational tasks while remaining equally responsible for technical/project work.

### b. Recorder / Secretary: **Charles Lutz**
* **Responsibilities:**
  * Take notes during meetings.
  * Distribute meeting minutes to all team members within **24 hours**.
* **Powers Granted:**
  * May request clarifications during meetings to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* **Expectation Changes:**
  * Ensures documentation is maintained without reducing their responsibility in other project areas.
